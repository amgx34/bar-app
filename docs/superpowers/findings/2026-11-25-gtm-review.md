## 2. The five things the owner must do before the first conversation

Ranked. "First conversation" means the first of the ten talks with 2Touch owners the founder already knows.

1. **Say yes, no or change to the eight decisions in `strategy.md` section 1, and to three rulings made in this review** (section 5.2 items 1 to 3): when the price is put in writing, whether the founder's own bar is one of the ten, and whether supplier reps are asked for introductions before counsel answers. Everything else hangs on these.
2. **Write down the truth about the founder's own bar.** Does it run Rail on real nights today? What may be said about it? The playbook's answer to "who else uses it?" is "my own bar runs it". If that is not true on the day, the sentence is a false statement to a prospect. Until this is written, say only "it is early and you would be among the first".
3. **Run the two real-data tips checks and the role-fix check on live, and rotate the keys that were printed.** These are owner steps already on the morning list. They do not block a conversation, but the conversations are about tips, and the founder should know before talking whether the tip figures hold on real nights.
4. **Hand the briefing to a lawyer, and decide the legal entity.** Until counsel answers: no calls, no texts, no email except a reply to someone who wrote first, no direct messages, and now no ask to a supplier rep either. In-person talks with owners the founder knows are the only live channel, which is enough for ten.
5. **Set up the sheet and learn the two scripts.** The Conversations tab in `metrics-sheet.md`, with three columns added (section 5.1 item 9). The discovery questions in playbook section 3 and the four price questions in pricing section 6, word for word. Never name a price, a range or a competitor's price.
