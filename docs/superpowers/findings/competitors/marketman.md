# MarketMan (marketman.com) - competitor record

All lines read on 2026-11-25. Sources: H = https://www.marketman.com (WebFetch summary), P = https://www.marketman.com/pricing (WebFetch summary), S = web search snippets (Capterra / Software Advice / SourceForge / Slashdot listings, third-party, lower confidence).

1. **Target.** Restaurants of all sizes, independents to multi-unit chains and franchises, "cafes, quick-service, full-service, and bars"; also food distributors and tech partners. [H] Bars are one of several segments, not the focus. One review says its phone counting suits high-volume bars with liquor inventory [S].
2. **Headline.** "Manage Inventory, Invoicing, Purchasing, Recipe Costing, and COGS, All from One Easy-to-Use Platform" [H]
3. **Nav.** Solutions, Pricing, Company, Blog, Resources; Login, Contact Sales, Contact Support, Request Demo [H]
4. **Pricing.** Tiers, monthly, with a custom-quote path [P]. Starter $249/mo (1 vendor EDI, 50 AI invoice scans/mo, recipe costing, AVT, waste); Growth $299/mo (smart ordering, order by recipe, 2 EDI, unlimited scans); Enterprise "From $449" (unit not stated; email invoice scanning, advanced reports, unlimited EDI, read-only API 5,000 calls/mo) [P]. Add-ons: API $25-$199/mo by location count; Commissary $499 or $749/mo [P]. Promo: "first 3 months free" year-end offer, terms not stated [H][P]. Capterra shows $249 as "Growth" and no free trial; SourceForge says a trial exists (conflict) [S]. Per-location vs per-account basis: unverified.
5. **Top features.** (a) Inventory counts and tracking; (b) recipe costing, menu profitability, actual-vs-theoretical (AVT) reporting; (c) smart ordering/purchasing plus invoice scanning and accounts payable. Also waste management. [H]
6. **Onboarding.** Sales-led: "Request Demo" / Contact Sales; no self-serve signup described. Single-location implementation typically 2-4 weeks with guided onboarding, data migration and staff training. Web-based, no hardware required. [H]
7. **Integrations named.** Lightspeed, TouchBistro, Toast, Square (POS); US Foods, Sysco, PFG (distributors); QuickBooks, Xero (accounting); "all major POS platforms" [H]. Aloha, Lavu also named by listings [S]. POS sync is one-way (POS to MarketMan) [S].
8. **Mobile.** Yes: iOS App Store and Google Play apps for ordering and counts on phone or tablet [H].
9. **Coverage vs Rail.**
   - Inventory/COGS: **yes** - [H]
   - Payroll and tip pooling: **no** (not mentioned on home or pricing; not exhaustively checked on feature pages) - [H][P]
   - Sales analytics (Z reports): **partial** - POS sales feed depletion, AVT and profitability reporting; no Z-report/daily sales analytics claimed - [H][S]
   - Supplier/rep ordering: **yes** - purchasing, vendor orders, EDI with distributors, smart ordering - [H][P]
10. **Dealbreaker candidates.** Contract length, cancellation, and onboarding/setup fees are not stated on the pricing page: `unverified` [P]. Pricing "can get pricey for small businesses" per one reviewer [S]. Plan gating: Starter limited to 1 EDI vendor and 50 scans; API and advanced reports cost extra [P]. No self-serve start, demo required [H]. One-way POS sync, manual 86ing [S].
