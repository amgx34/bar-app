# Sales playbook (G8)

Date: 2026-11-25. Branch `rebuild/foundation`. For one founder selling in person to independent bar owners who run the 2Touch POS, around Cincinnati and Northern Kentucky. Documents only: nothing here has been said, sent, printed or run.

**Do first.** In days 1 to 30 nothing is sold or installed: hold ten conversations with 2Touch owners the founder already knows, using section 3, and log them (section 10). No install at a stranger's bar until section 8 is ticked.

**How to use it.** Section 1 is the week. Sections 2 and 3 are what to say. Section 8 is the go/no-go gate: read it before any install at a stranger's bar. Every spoken line carries a claim id from `docs/go-to-market/claims-register.md` (written "reg A27"). If a line has no id it makes no product claim.

**Labels.** HYPOTHESIS: a belief nobody has tested, with its cheap test. UNVERIFIED: not confirmed. TODO(owner): only the owner can fill it. "We have not verified that" is a sentence the founder is allowed, and expected, to say out loud.

**The rules this playbook keeps**
- No cold calls, no cold texts, no cold email, no unsolicited direct messages. They are on hold (section 11). No script for them exists in this file.
- No testimonial, no saving, no setup time, no customer count, no price stated as final. The only reference is "the founder's own bar" (unnamed until the owner gives written permission and real numbers, strategy section 4 and plan open question 12).
- Never "the only". The wording is "we have not found another tool that does these together for a single bar" (reg B3, UNVERIFIED: say it only with "on the pages we read, 2026-11-25", and only if asked what makes Rail different).
- The 2Touch line ("Rail closes your night: the Z report, the tip split and the pay run, straight from the 2Touch you already own", reg B1) is used in conversation only, and only in the softer form in the claims register: "Rail reads your 2Touch Z report and works out the night's tip split and pay run for you to check and approve."
- Never say tips "reconcile to the Z report" to an owner until the two real-data checks are done (reg B5). Never say "pay staff correctly" (reg A24).

## 2. What to say

### 2.1 First visit: a quiet-hours walk-in

Goal: five minutes, or a time to come back. Not a sale.

**When.** Tuesday to Thursday afternoon, before service, when the bar is open to staff and deliveries. Do not interrupt a delivery, a count, or service. Do not keep pressing after a "no". Rules for in-person solicitation and liquor premises were not read: UNVERIFIED, so ask counsel (briefing section 2.1) and, if in doubt, leave when asked.

**Carry.** The leave-behind (section 9) once the print gate in section 9 is cleared; a phone with the demo bar open and logged in; nothing else. On a phone show demo steps 1, 4 and 5 only: the tip split and the pay run need a laptop until the phone layout is fixed (section 4).

**Opening (say it, then stop):**

> "Hi, I'm [founder name, TODO(owner)]. I built a small app for bars that run 2Touch. I'm not here to sell you anything today. Are you the person who does the tips and payroll, or is that someone else?"

If it is the owner or the person who works out tips and pay:

> "Could I have five minutes now, or is there a better time this week? If it's a bad time, I'll leave my details and go."

**The two-sentence reason (only if they say yes):**

> "Rail reads your 2Touch sales with a small program on your POS computer, and works out the night's tip split and pay run for you to check and approve (reg B1 softened, A27, A15). It does not file taxes or move your money; your bank does that (reg L1, L2)."

Then go straight to the first discovery question (section 3), not a pitch:

> "Could you show me how last night's tips were split?"

**If they say no or not now:** "No problem. Can I leave this and come back in a couple of weeks, or would you rather I didn't?" Honour the answer. If they say stop, write it down and do not return (counsel briefing 4.1).

**Close of the visit:** offer one of two next steps, never both: the demo on the phone right now (section 4), or a visit on a quiet afternoon to look at their close. Leave the one-page text (section 9).

**What not to say at the door:** any price; "free" without the pilot terms; any time saved; "everything you need"; "we work with your POS" without "2Touch"; anything about Toast; "easy" or "quick" about setup.

## 4. The demo flow on the demo bar

The demo bar is sample data (reg A6, A42, A43). It opens from the website, no signup, no card. It is cleared periodically (reg A42; a demo is deleted within about two days, reg P11). Show it on a laptop if possible.

**Say this before the first click:**

> "This is sample data, not a real bar. There is no POS program connected to it, because there's no real 2Touch behind it. At your bar the numbers would come from your own Z reports; here they're made up."

That is the honest limit, and the strongest line in the demo.

**Five steps (HYPOTHESIS that five suffices; test by whether owners ask to see more):**

1. **The night's sales.** Open a Z report night: sales, tip percentage, per-server sales (reg A16, softened). "This is what your 2Touch report becomes."
2. **The tip split.** Open the payroll day view for that night. Show pooled, individual and percentage-of-sales, a barback cut and an opener bonus (reg A15). Ask: "Which of these is how you do it?" Do not claim the figures match a Z report; that is not yet checked on real data (reg B5).
3. **The pay run.** Show a pay run being submitted, then an owner approving it before the bank file can be downloaded (reg B6, PARTLY). Say: "Rail never holds or moves your money; your bank does" (reg L2). **Only run this step once gate item 1 in section 8 is ticked;** the Payroll Review screen has defects today.
4. **Stock and the limit.** Show a POS sale reducing stock (reg B8, B20). Say the limit unprompted: "This only works once each item has a bottle size and a pour size or recipe set. That's real work at the start, so I leave stock until after your first approved pay run."
5. **Orders to reps.** Show an order to a rep by email or text and its history (reg A17).

**Close the demo with one question:** "What did you see that you'd want, and what did you see that wouldn't fit your bar?"

**What NOT to demo, and why:**
- **Toast, or any POS other than 2Touch.** Not claimed (reg A20, A21; `PRODUCT.md`). If asked: "You can import your exports by hand; a direct link is not ready."
- **Clover.** Code exists, never run on a real account (reg A22). Say "we have not verified that."
- **Taxes, W-2 or 1099 screens, or anything about withholding.** The withholding behind the figures is unchecked and switched off (reg A19). If asked: "Rail does not file or withhold tax" (reg L1).
- **Books, and any new-bar Sales or Inventory screen with no data.** They show zeros as if they were real (strategy section 8 items 5 and 6). The demo bar has data, so this is a caution about a new real bar.
- **The Payroll Review screen** until fixed (a "Confirm Payroll" button that saves nothing, an Approved badge over figures that recalculate: strategy section 8 item 1).
- **The payroll day view on a phone** (rows overlap: strategy section 8 item 8). Use a laptop.
- **The setup page or the front page** as proof of anything. Their promises of minutes, "under an hour", Toast and export or deletion are not backed (reg A20, A25, A28, A34).
- **Any made-up result,** such as "bars use this to save" or a figure from the hero card (reg A5 is sample data).

## 5. Objections and honest answers

Every answer uses only a verified fact, or says "we have not verified that." Where the owner is asked for a decision, the founder says what is not yet built.

**"What does it cost?" (price)**
> "I'm still setting the price with my first ten bars, so I'd like to hear what you'd expect to pay first. You'll have the price and the terms in writing before I install anything, and you won't be asked for a card to start."
Facts: the price is a HYPOTHESIS ($99 for the nightly close and $199 for the whole bar, `pricing-and-packaging.md` section 3), not stated aloud; no price goes on the website yet; the ladder for the first ten is in section 6. The written quote is handed over when the pilot is agreed and before the install (`pricing-and-packaging.md` section 4). If they compare to another tool: "I can't quote their price; their page may have changed. Check it." (`battlecards.md`: every competitor price is reported, dated, with a doubt).

**"I already use my POS reports."**
> "You should: Rail starts from the same data. What it adds is the tip split, the pay run you approve, and stock and rep orders on top, once set up. If your POS reports already do everything you need, you don't need me."
Basis: battlecard 11. Do not say the POS cannot do something; "not shown on the pages read" is the form for a competitor.

**"My accountant does payroll."**
> "That's fine. Rail doesn't file taxes, withhold taxes or pay anyone (reg L1). It works out the hours, tips and pay run for you to check and approve, and gives you a bank file or a figure to hand to your accountant. Your accountant keeps the filing."
Do not promise what an accountant login can see until the role fix is confirmed on live (reg B21; the role fix is item A1 in the overnight log, which is not register row A1). Do not claim a QuickBooks or Xero link (none exists).

**"I don't want a program touching my payroll money" (trust)**
> "Rail never holds or moves your money. It produces a file; you submit it to your own bank (reg L2). A pay run is frozen when it is submitted and an owner approves it before the bank file can be downloaded, unless an owner overrides and writes down why." (reg B6: say this only after gate item 1.) "Bank account numbers are encrypted, and only the last four digits show on screen" (reg A31, L3).
Honest add: "Rail is a small, founder-run company. I have no continuity plan to show you yet, and no full data export built; I can export your payroll and sales records by hand on request." (reg A34: do not say "export at any time".) TODO(owner): decide what the founder will commit to.

**"What if I switch and it goes wrong?" (switching risk)**
> "You don't switch your POS. Rail reads it with a read-only login and doesn't write to it (reg L6, E2). Keep doing your close your usual way alongside it for the pilot. If you stop, you've lost nothing."
Do not promise "no long-term contract" or "month to month" aloud until it is in writing (reg A39, B15). The pilot terms are in section 6. If they ask what the uninstaller removes: "We have not verified that; I'll check before you decide" (reg L7: the Terms and the Agent Licence disagree).

**"What if it's wrong?"**
> "It calculates figures for you to check and approve; the bar is still responsible for checking them, and the Terms say so (reg A24, Terms 3). At your first nights we compare every figure to how you did it by hand. If a number is wrong, I'll tell you, and I'll tell my other pilot bars."
Do not say "accurate", "correct" or "reconciles to your Z report" until the two real-data checks are done (reg B5). The metrics sheet's target for wrong money figures found by an owner is zero, and one is a stop.

**"Do I have to put a program on my POS computer?" (agent install)**
> "Yes. It's a small Windows service on the POS computer. It uses a read-only database login and never writes to your POS (reg E1, E2, L6). Setup needs someone with administrator rights, and I do the first installs in person with you there. I won't give you a setup time, because I haven't measured one with a real bar yet." (reg A25, A28)
Add, if asked about their 2Touch agreement: "I haven't verified what your 2Touch agreement says about a program reading its database. Please check, and give me your yes in writing before I install." (strategy section 3 risk 2; briefing Q6). Will it slow the POS? "We have not verified that. It reads every few minutes; I will watch it at install." (reg A27)

If a file is in a format Rail doesn't recognise, its text is sent to a third-party AI service to read it; the Privacy page says which and why (reg A32). We do not sell your data (reg L4)."

**"What happens if you disappear?"** See trust above: no continuity plan to show and no full export yet. Say it plainly. TODO(owner).

**"I'm not a computer person."**

## 7. Follow-up after a visit

Manual, one-to-one, relationship-based. No bulk messages, no sequences, no mail merge.

| When | What | Channel |
|---|---|---|
| Same day | Write the row in the sheet (section 10). Write one line of what the owner said in their own words. | Sheet |
| Within two days, only if the owner agreed to it | A short, hand-written message to the person who invited it: what was agreed (demo link, a visit date). No attachment unless asked. | The channel they used or named. In person if possible. |
| Day 14 | If the owner said "come back in a couple of weeks", return in person at a quiet hour. | In person |
| After a "no" | Nothing. Log the reason. Return only if they ask. | None |
| After "stop" | Stop on every channel and log it (briefing 4.1). | None |
| After a demo request reply with no answer | One reminder after seven days at most, hand-written. Then stop. | Email |
| After an introduction | Thank the introducer. Never bring up the introduced owner again unless that owner does. | In person |

Each follow-up message is typed to one person and mentions something that person said. A message that could go to any owner is not a follow-up; it is bulk, and is not used. Email to a prospect who has not asked for it is on hold (section 11).

HYPOTHESIS: a visit followed by a demo within two weeks lifts pilots per conversation. Test: after twenty conversations, compare pilots from those with a demo to those without.

> **Rail: one app for the whole bar** (reg B2)
>
> **What it does**
> - Reads your 2Touch sales with a small program on your POS computer, every few minutes. Windows only. (reg A27, B12)
> - Works out tip splits: pooled, individual or by percentage of sales, with barback cuts and opener bonuses, for you to check and approve. (reg A15)
> - Freezes a pay run when it is submitted; an owner approves it before the bank file can be downloaded. (reg B6; include this line only when Gate A item 1 is ticked)
> - Tracks stock by the bottle and keg, and reduces it when you sell, once each item has a bottle size and a pour size or recipe. (reg B8, B20)
> - Sends orders to your supplier reps by email or text and keeps a history. (reg A17)
> - Sends alerts once a day: low stock, nightly sales, unusual sales, a pay run waiting. (reg B9)

> **What it does not do**

> - It has no scheduling, no multi-location reporting, no accounting-software link, and no app-store app; it is a website you open on a phone or a computer. (strategy section 2)
> - Stock is not accurate until bottle and pour sizes are set. We start with the nightly close.

> **Your data**

> - The program reads your POS with a read-only login and does not write to it. (reg L6)
> - Rail does not sell your data. (reg L4)

## 10. What to log after each conversation

Write it the same day, in the Conversations tab of the sheet in `metrics-sheet.md`, no stranger's name:

| Column | Entry |
|---|---|
| date | the day of the conversation |
| channel | in person / phone / introduction (phone only when the prospect asked for the call; inbound replies by email go under "introduction" or "in person" as fits; note it) |
| 2Touch bar | yes or no |
| outcome | pilot / more info / no |
| reason if no | a few words (not a name) |
| the four price answers, if asked | cheap enough to doubt / a bargain / getting expensive / too expensive |

Two more things the founder keeps in a private notebook, not the sheet: the owner's own words about the pain (question 1 and question 5 in section 3), and any "stop" request with its date (briefing 4.1).

At the pilot stage the sheet's other tabs apply: install, first Z report, first approved pay run, night-31 invoice, and the Lost tab with one reason code.

**Weekly (Monday):** conversations held; pilots agreed; hours actually spent against the ten (HYPOTHESIS); wrong money figures an owner found; any bar lost with its reason.

### The stop rules (from the strategy and the metrics sheet; every threshold is a HYPOTHESIS)

| Rule | Action |
|---|---|
| Fewer than 5 pilots from 20 conversations | The segment or the lead offer is wrong. Test Clover bars, or lead with stock instead of the close, before more conversations. |
| Fewer than half of pilots reach an approved pay run within 14 days of the first Z report | Stop adding bars; fix onboarding (`onboarding-checklist.md`). Below ten pilots, report counts, not percentages. |
| More than 3 days from install to first synced Z report, two bars in a row | Stop adding bars; find the cause before the next install. |
| Any wrong money figure found by an owner | Stop; fix; tell the other pilots; then continue. |
| The same loss reason twice in the first ten | Fix it before the next pilot. |
| Warm introductions run dry before bar 5 | Test email, but only after counsel answers and a domain and postal address exist. Do not start calling. |
| Any prospect says stop | Stop on every channel; log it. |
| Two new bars in one week | Hold the third. One install at a time. |
